Client story

The listing Google wouldn’t unlock.

The salon had the hard part already — a full book, a 4.9-star reputation and a following built over years. What it did not have was any way to be found by someone who was not already a customer, because Google had flagged its Business Profile as a duplicate and locked it.

The situation

A real business, an unreachable one.

The salon serves two audiences who search in two different languages — locals in Swedish, the city’s students and visiting researchers in English. The site spoke both, but through a toggle that swapped the text at a single address. To a search engine, the English version did not exist.

The larger constraint sat outside the website. A duplicate flag on the Google Business Profile blocked verification and blocked every edit, and support answered for three weeks with the same instruction — verify first — about the profile Google had already locked. The reputation was live and public. The route to it was closed.

The work

What we did.

Two fronts at once: the profile work was worthless if the site could not convert the visit, and the site work was worthless while the profile stayed locked.

01

Two real sites in place of one bilingual page.

Five Swedish pages and five English pages at their own addresses, each with its own titles, descriptions, canonical tags and hreflang. A local and an English-speaking searcher now land on the right page instead of the same one, and salon structured data gives Google the hours, the service area and the booking route directly.

02

A site that loads fast and asks for nothing.

Modern image formats with fallbacks, explicit dimensions so nothing shifts as the page loads, and no analytics cookies or third-party pixels — which means no consent banner standing between a visitor and a booking, and a privacy position that holds without a lawyer.

03

The Google case taken to resolution.

When the support queue looped, we escalated through a second channel and got a product expert engaged on the record. That reframed the case from verification to ownership — the account holding the live listing was identified, and control of the profile customers actually see returned to the business.
